# Glossary

Intro: A field guide to the mycological, economic, and Web3 vocabulary used in
these pages. Terms are listed alphabetically; acronyms appear under the letter of
the term they abbreviate.

**Airdrop** — A free, broadcast distribution of tokens to many wallets at once. Mushrooms run nature’s oldest airdrop: spores, released by the billion and carried on the wind.

**Anthropocene** — The present geological epoch, in which human activity is the dominant force shaping climate, land, and ecosystems.

**Appeal to nature fallacy** — The mistaken assumption that whatever is natural must therefore be good for us. This book deliberately works around it.

**Arbitrage** — Profiting from the same resource being valued differently in two places. Fungi are nature’s arbitrageurs, moving nutrients from abundance toward scarcity.

**Biomimicry** — Design that borrows strategies already proven by evolution. MycoFi is economic biomimicry: mycelial patterns applied to human economies.

**Bioremediation** — Using living organisms — often fungi — to break down pollutants and restore damaged soil, water, or habitat.

**Blockchain** — A shared transaction ledger maintained by many independent computers, so that no single party controls the record.

**Bonding curve** — A smart contract that sets a token’s price as a mathematical function of its supply, minting and burning continuously instead of matching buyers to sellers.

**BUIDL** — Crypto slang, a deliberate misspelling of “build”, for the work of actually making things rather than speculating on price.

**Carbon sequestration** — Drawing carbon out of the atmosphere and storing it long-term. Mycelial networks route an estimated 13 billion tonnes of CO2 into soil each year.

**CoFi (Collaborative Finance)** — A movement to clear networked debts against one another, so that mutual obligations cancel out without cash changing hands.

**Community Asset Voucher (CAV)** — A voucher issued by a community and backed by its own future goods and services, enabling local trade without national currency.

**Complementary currency** — A means of exchange circulating alongside national money, usually created to serve needs the mainstream financial system ignores. Also called a community currency.

**Composability** — The property that lets independent contracts and protocols be plugged into one another like building blocks, without asking permission.

**Conviction Voting (CV)** — A governance mechanism in which support accrues the longer a participant backs a proposal, turning continuous preference into funding decisions.

**DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)** — An organization whose membership, treasury, and decision rules live in smart contracts rather than a corporate charter. Sub-DAOs nest inside one; meta-DAOs coordinate several.

**DeFi (Decentralized Finance)** — Lending, exchange, and other financial services rebuilt as open, permissionless protocols on public blockchains.

**Degen** — Self-deprecating crypto slang for a speculator chasing high-risk, high-return bets. Contrast with regen.

**Design pattern** — A reusable answer to a recurring problem, described generally enough to apply across many different contexts.

**Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)** — The broad category of shared, replicated record-keeping systems to which blockchains belong.

**Dynamic issuance** — Token supply that expands and contracts in response to real demand, rather than following a fixed, pre-set schedule.

**Ecological economics** — A school of economics that treats the economy as a subsystem of the biosphere, recognizing many forms of value rather than one.

**Emergence** — Coherent, coordinated behaviour arising out of simple local interactions, with no central controller directing it.

**Endogenous credit** — Credit an organization issues against its own productive capacity, instead of borrowing it from an external lender at a price.

**Endosymbiosis** — One organism living permanently inside another to mutual benefit. The mitochondria powering your cells are the classic result.

**Endosymbiotic finance** — MycoFi’s proposal that organizations internalize the functions of finance, much as early cells internalized the mitochondrion.

**Fiat currency** — State-issued money that is not redeemable for any commodity; its value rests on law, taxation, and confidence.

**Fluid democracy** — A system in which you may vote directly, delegate your vote to someone you trust, and re-delegate the moment that trust is broken. Also called liquid democracy.

**Fractal** — A form that repeats its own shape across scales, so that a small part resembles the whole. See self-similarity.

**Free rider problem** — When a participant draws on a shared resource without contributing to it. Mycelial networks detect free riders and cut them off.

**Fruiting body** — The mushroom itself: the reproductive structure a mycelial network pushes above ground in order to release spores.

**Funga** — The fungal kingdom, named alongside flora and fauna to give fungi equal ecological standing.

**Fungible / non-fungible** — Fungible units are interchangeable, as one dollar is with another; non-fungible ones are each unique. See NFT.

**Gitcoin Grants** — A public goods funding platform that distributes a matching pool to open-source and community projects using Quadratic Funding.

**Governance primitive** — A basic, reusable decision-making mechanism out of which larger governance systems can be assembled.

**Hypha (plural hyphae)** — The microscopic thread mycelium is built from: a chain of cells acting at once as pipe, sensor, and root.

**Indigenous Economics** — The study of indigenous resource-management practice and philosophy, grounded in reciprocity, kinship, and multigenerational responsibility. Also called Indigenomics.

**Institutional ecology** — The overlapping web of organizations, rules, and norms within which any single institution has to operate.

**Keystone species** — A species whose presence holds an entire ecosystem together. Mycelium is a keystone network beneath most life on land.

**Liquidity-saving mechanism** — Any arrangement that settles obligations using less cash, such as netting mutual debts before anyone pays.

**Meme** — A unit of culture that spreads by imitation. DeFi, ReFi, CoFi, and MycoFi are memes at least as much as they are technologies.

**Moloch** — Internet shorthand for coordination failure: the situation where everyone acts rationally and the collective outcome is one nobody wanted.

**Monoculture** — A system collapsed into a single variety. Applied to money, the crowding out of every other medium of exchange by one state currency.

**Mutual credit** — Trade settled by participants extending credit directly to one another, with balances across the network netting to zero.

**Mycelium (adjective mycelial)** — The underground body of a fungus: a branching network of hyphae that feeds, senses, remembers, and redistributes resources.

**MycoFi** — Mycelial finance. The practice of drawing economic and Web3 design patterns from mycelial networks — and the subject of this book.

**Mycology** — The scientific study of fungi.

**Mycopunk** — A regenerative subculture and aesthetic, sibling to solarpunk and lunarpunk, that takes fungi as its guiding metaphor. Compare lichenpunk.

**Mycorrhiza (adjective mycorrhizal)** — The symbiotic junction between fungal hyphae and plant roots, through which nutrients, sugars, and warning signals are traded.

**Neural Quorum Governance (NQG)** — A voting design in which you delegate not to one representative but to a weighted quorum, whose members carry influence according to expertise and history.

**NFT (Non-Fungible Token)** — A token representing a unique asset or claim. NFTs can be programmed to hold and govern other NFTs, nesting fractally.

**On-chain** — Recorded and executed on a blockchain, and therefore publicly verifiable by anyone.

**Permaculture** — Designing human systems on ecological principles: diversity, closed loops, minimal waste, and resilience measured in decades.

**Permissionless** — Open to anyone to join, use, or build on, with no gatekeeper and no prior approval.

**Physarum** — A slime mold widely used in research on decentralized problem-solving, best known for reproducing efficient rail-network designs.

**Polycentrism (adjective polycentric)** — Governance organized around many overlapping centres of authority rather than one, each fitted to its own scale and context.

**Positive-sum** — An interaction in which every party can come out ahead, as opposed to zero-sum, where one gains only at another’s expense.

**Public goods** — Resources anyone can use without using them up — roads, research, clean air, open-source code — and which markets chronically underfund.

**Puhpowee** — A Potawatomi word for the force with which a mushroom pushes up through the earth overnight.

**Quadratic Funding (QF)** — A matching formula that weights the number of contributors far more heavily than the size of their contributions, amplifying broad support.

**Quadratic Voting (QV)** — A voting system in which each additional vote on one issue costs progressively more, letting voters express how strongly they feel. Also called plural voting.

**Quorum sensing** — How organisms in a distributed population detect their own collective density and switch behaviour together, without any central signal.

**Radiotrophic** — Able to use ionizing radiation as an energy source. Some fungi thriving in the Chernobyl exclusion zone do exactly this.

**ReFi (Regenerative Finance)** — The turning of DeFi tooling toward ecological and social regeneration, especially the funding of public goods.

**Regen** — A builder oriented toward regeneration and long-term flourishing; the counterpart, and often the reformed version, of a degen.

**Rhizome** — A horizontal underground plant stem; by extension, any non-hierarchical network that has no single centre.

**Self-similarity** — The fractal property of looking essentially the same at every magnification, from hypha to neuron to galaxy.

**Smart contract** — Code deployed to a blockchain that executes automatically when its conditions are met, with no intermediary required.

**SME** — Small and medium-sized enterprise; the businesses that mutual credit clearing helps most in a cash-starved economy.

**Spore** — A single fungal cell packaged for dispersal, light enough to ride the wind for miles and capable of seeding an entirely new network.

**Symbiogenesis** — Evolution by merger: two organisms fusing permanently into one, as when a bacterium became the mitochondrion inside its host.

**Token engineering** — The discipline of designing, simulating, and stress-testing token-based economic systems before they are deployed into the world.

**Total Value Locked (TVL)** — The standard DeFi metric for capital parked inside a protocol. This book proposes Total Value Flowed — circulation, not accumulation — in its place.

**UBI (Universal Basic Income)** — An unconditional income paid to everyone, sufficient to cover basic needs, regardless of work or wealth.

**Web3** — The generation of internet applications built on blockchains, in which users hold their own identity, data, and assets.

**Wood-wide-web** — Biologists’ nickname for the mycelial network that connects more than 90 percent of the world’s trees and plants underground.
